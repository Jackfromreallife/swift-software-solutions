## 1. 设计原则 / Design Principles

| # | 原则 | Principle |
|---|------|-----------|
| 1 | **先评估，后分级**：每位学员入学前都完成基础评估，按结果安排起始级别。 | **Assess before placing**: every new player completes a baseline assessment before being assigned a starting level. |
| 2 | **技能主线贯穿多级**：同一技能（如正手高远球）在多个级别反复出现，每升一级要求更高（移动、对角、压力下、隐蔽）。 | **Skills run across levels**: the same skill (e.g. forehand clear) comes back at several levels, with a higher standard each time (movement, cross-court, under pressure, disguise). |
| 3 | **新学 + 巩固**：每级技能分为"新学"（首次出现）和"巩固进阶"（前级技能提高要求）。 | **New + Reinforce**: each level has new skills (first appearance) and reinforce skills (earlier skills at a higher standard). |
| 4 | **可观察、可量化**：考核标准尽量用"10 球中几球""连续几拍"等可数的指标。 | **Observable & measurable**: criteria use countable targets ("x out of 10", "y-shot rally"). |
| 5 | **少儿重乐趣与基础动作**，参考 LTAD（长期运动员发展）"FUNdamentals / 学会训练"阶段。 | **Kids: fun + fundamental movement**, following LTAD "FUNdamentals" and "Learn to Train" stages. |
| 6 | **成人重效率与防伤**：纠正动作、提升步法效率、比赛应用。 | **Adults: efficiency & injury prevention**: technique correction, efficient footwork, match application. |
| 7 | 技能分为**技术、步法、战术、体能、心理/规则**五大维度。 | Skills span 5 domains: **Technical, Footwork, Tactical, Physical, Mental/Rules**. |

## 2. 框架总览 / Framework Overview

### 2.1 级别对照 / Level Map

两个版本共用同一套 6 级结构，便于少儿学员成年后无缝衔接。
Both tracks share the same 6-level spine, so kids can move into the adult track without re-mapping.

| 级别 Level | 中文名称 | English Name | 少儿版徽章 Kids Badge | 成人版定位 Adult Profile | 大致对应 Rough Equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **L1** | 启蒙级 | Foundation | 🐣 小雏鸟 Chick | 零基础 / 刚接触 Absolute beginner | 能偶尔击中球 Can occasionally hit the shuttle |
| **L2** | 入门级 | Beginner | 🐦 小飞燕 Swallow | 会打但无规范动作 Casual player, no formal technique | 能简单对打 Can rally casually |
| **L3** | 基础级 | Improver | 🦅 雏鹰 Young Eagle | 动作初步规范 Basic correct technique | 能打满底线、懂规则 Clears to baseline, knows rules |
| **L4** | 进阶级 | Intermediate | 🦅 雄鹰 Eagle | 社区/俱乐部中等 Club intermediate | 能稳定四方球、有简单战术 Consistent 4-corner game, basic tactics |
| **L5** | 提高级 | Advanced | 🔥 猎鹰 Falcon | 俱乐部高水平 Strong club player | 能参加地区业余比赛 Competes in local amateur events |
| **L6** | 竞技级 | Competitive | 🏆 金翅鹏 Golden Roc | 业余高手 / 青少年竞技 Top amateur / junior competitive | 能参加省市级比赛 Ready for regional/provincial events |

> 💡 建议：L1–L3 每级约 3–6 个月；L4 之后每级 6–12 个月（以每周 2 次课计）。
> Suggested duration: L1–L3 ≈ 3–6 months per level; L4+ ≈ 6–12 months (based on 2 sessions/week).

### 2.3 技能主线 / Skill Threads

技能不是"学完就结束"。每条技能主线从某一级开始（🆕 新学），之后每一级都会再次出现并提高要求（🔁 巩固进阶）。早期级别会列出"下一级预告"，让学员看到这项技能未来的方向。
Skills are never "finished". Each skill thread starts at one level (🆕 New) and returns at every later level with a higher standard (🔁 Reinforce). Early levels show a "next level preview" so players can see where each skill is heading.

**例 Example — 正手高远球 Forehand clear：**

| 级别 | 要求 Standard |
|---|---|
| L1 | 喂球下原地击球过网 Static hit over the net from a hand-feed |
| L2 | 原地击到后场 Static clear to the rear court |
| L3 | 移动后击到后场 1/3 Clear to the rear third after moving |
| L4 | 四方移动中直线 + 对角 Straight and cross-court during 4-corner movement |
| L5 | 防守高远与进攻平高（含头顶） Defensive and flat attacking clears, incl. round-the-head |
| L6 | 隐蔽一致、比赛节奏、起跳 Disguised, match tempo, jumping |

完整的主线总览见各版本第 5 / 6 节开头。 See the thread overview at the start of sections 5 and 6.

## 3. 技能达成等级 / Skill Achievement Grades

Grades are measured **against the current level's standard**: a skill at ⭐⭐⭐ in L2 is rated again from ⭐ against the harder L4 standard.

| 档位 Grade | 中文 | English | 定义 Definition |
|---|---|---|---|

## 4. 训练前基础评估 / Pre-Training Assessment

### 4.1 评估流程 / Assessment Flow

```
① 信息登记与健康筛查  →  ② 身体素质/动作筛查  →  ③ 技术测试站  →  ④ 对打/比赛观察  →  ⑤ 综合评分定级  →  ⑥ 反馈与目标设定
① Intake & health screen → ② Physical/movement screen → ③ Technical stations → ④ Rally/match observation → ⑤ Scoring & placement → ⑥ Feedback & goals
```

总时长约 **45–60 分钟**（少儿约 40 分钟）。
Total time ≈ **45–60 min** (kids ≈ 40 min).

### 4.2 第①步：信息登记与健康筛查 / Step ①: Intake & Health Screen

| 项目 Item | 少儿版 Kids | 成人版 Adults |
|---|---|---|
| 基本信息 Basic info | 年龄、身高、惯用手、学校 Age, height, handedness, school | 年龄、职业、惯用手 Age, occupation, handedness |
| 球龄与经历 Experience | 是否学过、多久、其他运动 Prior lessons, duration, other sports | 打球年限、频率、是否比赛 Years played, frequency, competition history |
| 健康 Health | 家长填写：哮喘、过敏、伤病 Parent form: asthma, allergies, injuries | PAR-Q 运动风险问卷；膝、肩、腰、跟腱旧伤 PAR-Q; knee/shoulder/back/Achilles history |
| 目标 Goals | 兴趣 / 体能 / 校队 / 比赛 Fun / fitness / school team / competition | 健身 / 社交 / 提高水平 / 比赛 Fitness / social / improve / compete |
| 时间投入 Commitment | 每周可训练次数 Sessions per week | 每周可训练次数 Sessions per week |

> ⚠️ 成人如 PAR-Q 任一项为"是"，建议先咨询医生再进行体能测试。
> ⚠️ If an adult answers "yes" to any PAR-Q item, recommend medical clearance before physical testing.

### 4.4 第③步：技术测试站 / Step ③: Technical Stations

每站 10 球，记录成功数。少儿 8 岁以下可用短拍、降低网高或缩小场地。
10 shuttles per station; record successes. For kids under 8: short racket, lower net, or mini court.

| 站 Station | 测试 Test | 方法 Method | 成功标准 Success Criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1** | 握拍检查 Grip check | 教练观察正反手握拍与转换 Observe forehand/backhand grip & switching | 观察打分 1–4 Observed 1–4 |
| **T2** | 颠球 Shuttle keep-up | 正手连续颠球（少儿）/ 正反手交替（成人） Forehand keep-ups (kids) / alternating (adults) | 少儿 Kids ≥10 次; 成人 Adults ≥20 次 alternating |
| **T3** | 正手高远发球 Forehand high serve | 发至后场双打后发球线至底线区域 Into zone between doubles long-service line and baseline | 落区内 In zone |
| **T4** | 反手网前发球 Backhand low serve | 过网高度 <30cm，落前发球线后 1m 内 Clears net by <30cm, lands within 1m of short service line | 过网低且落区内 Low & in zone |
| **T5** | 正手高远球 Forehand clear | 教练喂高球，回击至后场 1/3 Coach feeds high; return to rear third | 落后场 1/3 Rear third |
| **T6** | 网前放网 Net shot | 教练手抛网前，放网落前发球线内 Hand-feed at net; land inside short service line | 不冒高、落区内 Tight & in zone |
| **T7** | 挑后场 Underarm lift | 网前接球挑至后场 1/3 Lift from net to rear third | 落后场 1/3 Rear third |
| **T8** | 杀球（仅成人/L3+ 少儿） Smash (adults / kids L3+) | 喂半场高球，向下杀至中后场 Feed mid-high; smash downward | 明显下压且入界 Steep & in |
| **T9** | 连续对拉 Rally count | 与教练连续对拉高远球 Continuous clear rally with coach | 记录最高连续拍数 Max consecutive shots |

### 4.5 第④步：对打 / 比赛观察 / Step ④: Rally & Match Observation

- 与同水平学员或教练打 1 局 11 分（少儿可打 7 分）。
  Play one 11-point game (kids: 7 points) vs. a peer or coach.
- 教练按下表观察打分 1–4 / Coach rates 1–4 on:

| 观察项 Item | 1 分 | 4 分 |
|---|---|---|
| 准备与回位 Ready & recovery | 击球后站着不动 Stands still after hitting | 每拍后回中并有启动 Recovers to base with split step |
| 线路意识 Placement | 只打对方身上 Hits to opponent | 有意调动四角 Deliberately moves opponent to corners |
| 规则与计分 Rules & scoring | 不清楚 Unclear | 能独立计分、判断界内外 Scores and calls lines independently |
| 比赛态度 Attitude | 易放弃 / 情绪化 Gives up / emotional | 专注、积极 Focused, positive |

### 4.6 第⑤步：综合评分与定级 / Step ⑤: Scoring & Placement

**总分计算 Total score（满分 100）：**

| 部分 Section | 权重 Weight | 计算 Calculation |
|---|---|---|
| 身体/动作筛查 Physical screen | 20% | 6 项均分 ÷ 4 × 20 Avg of 6 items ÷ 4 × 20 |
| 技术测试站 Technical stations | 50% | T3–T8 成功率均值 × 50（T1/T2/T9 作参考加减分） Avg success rate T3–T8 × 50 (T1/T2/T9 as ± adjustment) |
| 对打观察 Match observation | 30% | 4 项均分 ÷ 4 × 30 Avg of 4 items ÷ 4 × 30 |

**定级参考 Placement guide：**

| 总分 Score | 起始级别 Start Level | 备注 Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0–25 | **L1** 启蒙 Foundation | 无法连续击球 Cannot rally |
| 26–45 | **L2** 入门 Beginner | 能简单对打 Basic rally |
| 46–60 | **L3** 基础 Improver | 动作基本正确 Mostly correct technique |
| 61–75 | **L4** 进阶 Intermediate | 四方球较稳定 Fairly consistent 4-corner |
| 76–88 | **L5** 提高 Advanced | 需加测 L5 技能 Run L5 skill check |
| 89–100 | **L6** 竞技 Competitive | 需加测 + 实战 Extra test + match vs. L6 player |

> 📌 分数只作参考，**教练有最终调整权**（±1 级）。存在明显短板（如完全不会反手）时，就低定级，并在个人计划中标注。
> 📌 Score is a guide; **head coach may adjust ±1 level**. If there is a major gap (e.g. no backhand at all), place lower and flag it in the individual plan.

### 4.7 第⑥步：反馈与目标设定 / Step ⑥: Feedback & Goals

- 当场给出：**2 个优点 + 2 个优先提升项**。
  Give on the spot: **2 strengths + 2 priority improvements**.
- 设定 8–12 周目标（SMART），写入学员档案。
  Set an 8–12 week SMART goal in the player record.
- 少儿版：给家长一页纸报告 + 徽章卡。
  Kids: one-page parent report + badge card.

## 7. 升级考核规则 / Level-Up Rules

| 规则 | Rule |
|---|---|
| **核心要求**：本级所有技能（新学 + 巩固）按本级标准达到 ⭐⭐⭐ 稳定。 | **Core**: every skill in the level (new and reinforce) at ⭐⭐⭐ against this level's standard. |
| **应用要求**：至少 **3 项**（L1–L2 为 2 项）达到 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 运用。 | **Application**: at least **3 skills** (2 for L1–L2) at ⭐⭐⭐⭐. |
| **比赛要求**：L3 以上需在比赛观察中由 2 名教练确认。 | **Match**: from L3 up, confirmed by 2 coaches in match observation. |
| **考核周期**：每 8 周一次阶段评估，每 3–6 个月一次升级考核。 | **Cycle**: progress check every 8 weeks; level-up test every 3–6 months. |
| **不能升级时**：给出未达标技能清单 + 下一阶段训练重点。 | **If not passed**: list unmet skills + next training focus. |
| **少儿额外**：发放徽章证书，家长可在场观看。 | **Kids extra**: badge certificate; parents may attend. |
