## 1. 双打设计原则 / Doubles Design Principles

| # | 原则 | Principle |
|---|------|-----------|
| 1 | **单打打基础，双打练配合**：击球技术主要在单打框架中学习，双打框架重点在发接发、平抽、站位轮转和配合。 | **Singles builds strokes, doubles builds teamwork**: core strokes are learned in the singles framework; doubles focuses on serve/return, flat game, formation, rotation and partnership. |
| 2 | **准入要求**：进入双打 Ln 级，单打需至少达到 L(n−1) 级。 | **Entry rule**: to enter doubles level Ln, the player should be at least singles level L(n−1). |
| 3 | **双打从 L2 开始正式训练**；L1 只做两人合作类游戏。 | **Formal doubles starts at L2**; L1 uses cooperative partner games only. |
| 4 | **个人评分 + 组合观察**：技能按个人打分，比赛观察同时记录组合表现。 | **Individual grades + pair observation**: skills are graded per player; match observation also notes the pair's performance. |
| 5 | **核心理念"抢先下压"**：双打强调主动进攻、不轻易起高球。 | **Core idea "attack first, keep it down"**: doubles rewards taking the attack and avoiding unnecessary lifts. |
| 6 | **少儿重合作与沟通**，**成人重轮转与战术默契**。 | **Kids: cooperation & communication**; **Adults: rotation & tactical understanding**. |
| 7 | **技能主线贯穿多级**：与单打相同，每项双打技能从某一级开始（🆕 新学），之后每级提高要求（🔁 巩固进阶）。 | **Skills run across levels**: as in singles, each doubles skill starts at one level (🆕 New) and returns with a higher standard at every later level (🔁 Reinforce). |

## 3. 技能达成等级 / Skill Achievement Grades

与单打相同，按**本级标准**评价；升级后同一技能按新一级的更高标准重新评。
As in singles, grades are measured **against the current level's standard**; after levelling up, the same skill is rated again against the harder standard.

| 档位 Grade | 中文 | English | 双打中的解读 Doubles Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **1** | 认识 | Aware | 教练喂球 / 讲解下能做出动作或站到位 Can do it with feed or explanation |
| ⭐⭐ **2** | 会做 | Developing | 2 对 1 或 2 对 2 配合练习中约 50% 成功 ~50% in cooperative 2v1 / 2v2 drills |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **3** | 稳定 | Consistent | 对抗练习中 ≥ 70%，且和搭档配合不乱 ≥70% in competitive drills, without clashing with partner |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **4** | 运用 | Applied | 比赛中主动、合理使用，且能与**不同搭档**配合 Used purposefully in matches, with **different partners** |

> 💡 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 的"不同搭档"要求：至少与 2 位搭档比赛中观察到，避免只和固定搭档才打得好。
> 💡 The "different partners" rule for ⭐⭐⭐⭐: observe with at least 2 partners, so the skill isn't tied to one regular partner.

## 4. 双打基础评估 / Doubles Pre-Training Assessment

### 4.1 评估流程 / Assessment Flow

双打评估作为单打评估的**加测模块**（约 25–30 分钟）。已做过单打评估的学员，第①②步结果直接沿用。
The doubles assessment is an **add-on module** (≈ 25–30 min). If the player already did the singles assessment, reuse steps ① and ②.

```
①② 沿用单打：信息登记 + 身体筛查  →  ③ 双打技术站  →  ④ 双打比赛观察  →  ⑤ 综合评分定级  →  ⑥ 反馈与搭档建议
①② Reuse singles: intake + physical → ③ Doubles stations → ④ Doubles match observation → ⑤ Scoring & placement → ⑥ Feedback & partner advice
```

> 如学员**只打双打**、未做单打评估，需先完成单打评估的第①②步和技术站 T1–T7。
> If the player **only plays doubles** and has no singles assessment, first complete singles steps ①②, and stations T1–T7.

### 4.2 第③步：双打技术站 / Step ③: Doubles Technical Stations

每站 10 球，记录成功数。 10 shuttles per station; record successes.

| 站 Station | 测试 Test | 方法 Method | 成功标准 Success Criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DT1** | 反手网前发球（双打） Backhand low serve (doubles) | 发至对方前发球线附近，左右区各 5 球 5 per service court, to the short service line | 过网 <20cm（少儿 <30cm）且落前发球线后 50cm 内 Clears net by <20cm (kids <30cm), within 50cm of short line |
| **DT2** | 平高球发球 Flick serve | 发至双打后发球线附近 To the doubles long service line | 过接发者拍头且入界 Over receiver's reach & in |
| **DT3** | 接发球 Return of short serve | 教练发网前球，学员推/扑/放至 3 个目标区 Coach serves short; player pushes/kills/nets to 3 target zones | 落目标区且不起高 In zone & not lifted |
| **DT4** | 平抽对抽 Drive exchange | 与教练半场平抽，计连续拍数 Mid-court drive rally with coach | 记录最高连续拍数 Max consecutive shots |
| **DT5** | 接杀防守 Smash defence | 教练从后场杀球，学员挡网 / 挑底线 / 反抽 Coach smashes; player blocks / lifts deep / counters | 回球到位 Quality return |
| **DT6** | 前场封网 Net interception | 教练从中后场平推，学员在网前拦截下压 Coach pushes flat; player intercepts and hits down at net | 下压且入界 Downward & in |
| **DT7** | 连续杀球（仅成人/L3+ 少儿） Repeated smash (adults / kids L3+) | 教练连续挑 6 个后场球 Coach lifts 6 in a row | 杀球下压且回位 Downward with recovery |

### 4.3 第④步：双打比赛观察 / Step ④: Doubles Match Observation

- 学员与教练/助教组队，对阵一对同水平学员，打 1 局 11 分（少儿 7 分）。如条件允许，中途换一次搭档。
  Pair the player with a coach/assistant vs. a peer pair; one 11-point game (kids: 7). Swap partner once if possible.
- 教练按下表打分 1–4 / Coach rates 1–4:

| 观察项 Item | 1 分 | 4 分 |
|---|---|---|
| 站位意识 Formation | 两人站在一起或乱跑 Stands with partner or wanders | 进攻前后、防守左右，转换自然 Front-back on attack, side-by-side on defence, smooth switch |
| 主动下压 Keeping it down | 经常无故起高球 Lifts often without need | 尽量平推/下压，抢攻意识强 Keeps it flat/down, seeks attack |
| 沟通配合 Communication | 不说话，中路球撞拍或让球 Silent; clashes or leaves middle shuttles | 有口令，中路球分工清楚 Calls clearly; middle shuttles sorted |
| 规则 Rules | 不清楚发球区与轮换 Unclear on service courts/order | 能独立判断双打发球顺序 Manages doubles service order independently |
| 态度与鼓励 Attitude | 埋怨搭档 Blames partner | 互相鼓励、失误后积极 Encourages partner, positive after errors |

### 4.4 第⑤步：综合评分与定级 / Step ⑤: Scoring & Placement

**总分计算 Total score（满分 100）：**

| 部分 Section | 权重 Weight | 计算 Calculation |
|---|---|---|
| 身体筛查（沿用单打） Physical screen (from singles) | 15% | 6 项均分 ÷ 4 × 15 |
| 双打技术站 Doubles stations | 45% | DT1–DT3、DT5–DT7 成功率均值 × 45（DT4 作参考加减分） Avg success DT1–DT3, DT5–DT7 × 45 (DT4 as ± adjustment) |
| 双打比赛观察 Match observation | 40% | 5 项均分 ÷ 4 × 40 |

> 双打的比赛观察权重高于单打（40% vs 30%），因为站位、沟通等能力只能在实战中看到。
> Match observation weighs more than in singles (40% vs 30%) because formation and communication only show in live play.

**定级参考 Placement guide：**

| 总分 Score | 建议双打级别 Suggested Doubles Level |
|---|---|
| 0–25 | **L1** 启蒙 Foundation |
| 26–45 | **L2** 入门 Beginner |
| 46–60 | **L3** 基础 Improver |
| 61–75 | **L4** 进阶 Intermediate |
| 76–88 | **L5** 提高 Advanced（需加测 extra check） |
| 89–100 | **L6** 竞技 Competitive（需加测 + 实战 extra check + match） |

> 📌 **最终级别 = min（评分建议级别，单打级别 + 1）**，教练可再调整 ±1 级。
> 📌 **Final level = min(score-based level, singles level + 1)**; head coach may adjust ±1.

### 4.5 第⑥步：反馈与搭档建议 / Step ⑥: Feedback & Partner Advice

- 2 个优点 + 2 个优先提升项 / 2 strengths + 2 priorities.
- **场区倾向**：建议更适合前场还是后场（只作参考，L4 前两种都要练）。
  **Court preference**: note whether the player leans front court or rear court (for reference only — train both until L4).
- **搭档建议**：推荐水平相近、风格互补的搭档。
  **Partner advice**: suggest partners of similar level with complementary styles.

## 7. 升级考核规则 / Level-Up Rules

| 规则 | Rule |
|---|---|
| **核心要求**：本级所有双打技能（新学 + 巩固）按本级标准达到 ⭐⭐⭐ 稳定。 | **Core**: every doubles skill in the level (new and reinforce) at ⭐⭐⭐ against this level's standard. |
| **应用要求**：至少 **3 项**（L1–L2 为 2 项）达到 ⭐⭐⭐⭐，且与 ≥ 2 位搭档观察到。 | **Application**: at least **3 skills** (2 for L1–L2) at ⭐⭐⭐⭐, observed with ≥ 2 partners. |
| **单打准入**：升到双打 Ln 前，单打需达到 L(n−1)。 | **Singles entry**: before moving up to doubles Ln, singles must be at L(n−1). |
| **比赛要求**：L3 以上需 2 名教练在比赛中确认。 | **Match**: from L3 up, confirmed by 2 coaches in match play. |
| **个人升级**：升级按个人评定，**搭档不必同时升级**。 | **Individual**: players level up individually; **partners don't have to move up together**. |
| **考核周期**：每 8 周阶段评估，每 3–6 个月升级考核。 | **Cycle**: progress check every 8 weeks; level-up test every 3–6 months. |
